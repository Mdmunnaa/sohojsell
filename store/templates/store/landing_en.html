FAQ

Questions sellers ask us

Is {{ site.name }} in English?

Your online store can be fully in English — product pages, cart, checkout, order tracking, invoices and the customer SMS. You choose English or Bangla in Website → Design. The seller dashboard (POS, orders, reports) is in Bangla, so shop owners and staff across Bangladesh can use it without training.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Everything works from your phone. Adding products, checking orders and billing at the POS is no harder than using Facebook. If you get stuck, we're on WhatsApp.

Do I need to buy a domain or hosting?

No. Your store goes live at yourshop.{{ site.store_domain }} as soon as you sign up — hosting is included. If you want your own domain (www.yourshop.com) later, you can add it on the Premium plan.

How do customers pay?

Cash on delivery is the most common — the courier collects the money and pays you. You can also take advance payment with bKash, Nagad or card (the full amount, or just the delivery charge).

How does courier booking work?

Add the API key from your Steadfast, Pathao or RedX merchant account in settings. Then book a parcel with one click from the order; the tracking code is saved to the order and the customer can follow it on the tracking page.

How much does SMS cost?

SMS is separate from your plan — you buy only what you need{% if sms_rate %}, at ৳{{ sms_rate|floatformat:2 }} per SMS{% endif %}. Each automatic order SMS can be turned on or off, so nothing is wasted. English SMS fit 160 characters in one message, so they cost less than Bangla.

What happens after the {{ trial_days }}-day trial?

Pick a plan and pay by bKash, Nagad or card, and you continue with all your data. If you don't, the dashboard pauses but your website stays open for 7 more days so no customer order is lost. We never take a card up front, so you are never charged automatically.

Is my data safe?

Your shop's data is visible only from your account, and you can give staff access to just the parts they need. Every page runs on HTTPS. You can download your customers and orders to Excel at any time.
